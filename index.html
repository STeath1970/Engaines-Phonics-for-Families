Welcome!

Phonics helps children learn how letters and sounds work together. At Engaines, we use phonics to help children read words, write words and become confident readers.

You do not need to be a phonics expert. Short, happy practice is much more useful than long sessions. Use the videos and activities on this page whenever you need them.

Questions to ask as you listen

For the rhymes: Can you clap the rhythm of the rhyme? Can you tell me which words rhyme?

For the stories: Which character would you like to be? Why? Where do you think this story is happening? How do you think the character feels at the beginning of the story? How do they feel at the end?

Listen together.
Sing, clap and join in with your child.
Keep it playful.
There is no need to turn every song into a lesson.
Repeat favourites.
Repetition helps children remember sounds and words.

A few simple reminders

  • Keep practice short. A few minutes can make a difference.
  • Say sounds clearly. Avoid adding an extra “uh” sound where possible.
  • Blend sounds together. Help your child push the sounds together to read the word.
  • Celebrate effort. Mistakes are part of learning.
  • Ask us. If you are unsure, use the Padlet or speak to the Reception team.
